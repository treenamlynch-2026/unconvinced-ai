Privacy Policy

Congress Trade Detective · Last updated October 28, 2026

Congress Trade Detective is published by Treena Lynch (Unconvinced.). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account or login, and it does not collect, store, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.

Data stored on your device

Your watchlist and alert settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party data sources

To show trades, prices and news, the app requests public data from third-party providers. These requests contain no account or identity information. Like any web request, a provider may see your device's IP address under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
