Congress Trade Detective

See what members of Congress are trading.

Congress Trade Detective is a free iPhone app that tracks stock trades members of Congress disclose under the STOCK Act. It shows who traded, what, when, and how the stock moved before and after the trade became public. No login and no tracking.

Download on the App StoreSupport: inquiry@unconvinced.ai

Features

  • Trade feed of recent House and Senate disclosures: member, ticker, buy or sell, amount range, trade date and disclosure date
  • Two numbers per trade: price move since the trade, and since it became public
  • Price charts with the trade marked, company news and related congressional activity
  • Watchlist and alerts for tickers you follow
  • Shareable Case File cards
  • Ask Hal, the app's robot detective

Common questions

What is Congress Trade Detective?
An iPhone app that collects the stock trades members of Congress publicly disclose under the STOCK Act.
How can I track stock trades by members of Congress?
Congress Trade Detective shows House and Senate trade disclosures in one feed, with charts and alerts.
What is the STOCK Act?
A U.S. law requiring members of Congress to publicly disclose their stock trades.
Is the app free?
Yes. It is free, with no login and no data collection.
Where does the data come from?
Trade data comes from public financial disclosure filings; congressional activity from public records; news from third-party providers.
Is this financial advice?
No. It is an informational tool and does not accuse anyone of wrongdoing. Filings can be delayed, amended, or reported as value ranges.
