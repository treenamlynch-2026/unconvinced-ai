Snapshot Puzzle: Photo Jigsaw

Turn your own photos into jigsaw puzzles.

Snapshot Puzzle is a free jigsaw puzzle maker for iPhone and iPad that turns your own photos into puzzles of 25 to 1,024 pieces. No ads, no account, works offline, and your photos never leave your device.

Download on the App StoreSupport

Features

  • Make a custom jigsaw puzzle from any photo in your library
  • Choose 25, 100, 256, 500 or 1,024 pieces
  • Sort pieces into 4 drawers to organize edges, colors and sections
  • Auto-Separate spreads overlapping pieces; Reset Scatter reshuffles the table
  • Zoom for precise placement on iPhone and iPad
  • Hints on or off, plus a pausable solve timer
  • 15 table surfaces and a white or black board
