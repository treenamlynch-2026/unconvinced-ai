The Cut Card

A blackjack trainer for players who want to get good.

The Cut Card is a free blackjack trainer for iPhone. Play full multi-deck shoes with real cut-card reshuffles, always-on basic strategy, and a shoe tracker for practicing card counting. Points only, no real money.
